About Jyotish (Vedic Astrology)
Jyotish — "Science of Light" — is one of the six Vedāṅgas (limbs of the Vedas), with a documented tradition spanning over 3,500 years. Unlike Western astrology, Jyotish uses the sidereal zodiac (fixed stars), which is approximately 23° behind the tropical zodiac used in the West. This means your Vedic Sun sign often differs from your Western Sun sign.

The cornerstone of Jyotish is the Navagraha — nine celestial bodies including Rahu and Ketu (the lunar nodes) — whose positions at birth and transits through your life are analyzed through the Vimshottari Dasha (planetary period) system, a 120-year cycle that precisely times life events. This is the system that predicts marriage years, career peaks, and karmic turning points.
