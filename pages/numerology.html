About your Life Path Number

What is a Life Path Number?

It's the single most important number in numerology — the theme of your whole life. It's calculated from your date of birth alone, so you always have it, whatever your name.

How is it calculated?

We reduce your birth year, month, and day each to a single digit, add them, then reduce again — the standard Pythagorean method. Master numbers 11, 22, and 33 are kept as-is.

What's the difference from my Expression & Soul Urge numbers?

Those come from the letters of your full birth name: Expression = all letters (your outer talents), Soul Urge = the vowels (what your heart wants). Add your name above to see them.

Is this free?

Yes — your Life Path Number and its reading are free, every day. The full profile is part of Runae membership.
