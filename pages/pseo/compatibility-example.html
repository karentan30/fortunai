The Dragon–Rat dynamic

The Dragon brings ambition, charisma, and a big-picture vision; the Rat brings strategy, resourcefulness, and sharp timing. Together they belong to the same affinity trine (Rat–Dragon–Monkey), which the tradition reads as naturally cooperative. In practice, the Rat sees the angles the Dragon is too proud to notice, and the Dragon gives the Rat a mission worth the effort.

Where they click

  • Shared drive — both want to get somewhere, so neither drags the other down.
  • Complementary roles — Dragon leads the front, Rat runs the strategy.
  • Mutual respect for competence — each admires what the other does well.

Where the friction is

Both animals are strong-willed and status-aware. The friction appears when ambition competes instead of combines — two people wanting to steer the same ship. It usually resolves once roles are named and the goal is shared rather than personal.
