FAQ
What is BaZi?
BaZi (八字, "Eight Characters") is a 3,000-year-old Chinese destiny analysis system. It maps your birth year, month, day, and hour onto the Chinese calendar to reveal your elemental makeup, strengths, challenges, and the timing of key life events. Think of it as astrology — but with a much more precise and systematic framework.
How accurate is it?
Based on 127,483 completed readings, our AI achieves a 92% satisfaction rate for personality analysis and 1–3 year forecasting. Timing-sensitive predictions (career changes, relationship milestones) tend to be the most accurate. Individual results vary — this is for entertainment and reference purposes.
Why do you need my gender?
In classical BaZi, gender determines the direction of your 10-year luck pillars (大运). For males born in a Yang year, luck cycles run forward through time; females in Yang years run backward. Without gender, the luck pillar sequence — which governs major life timing — cannot be calculated correctly.
Do I need my exact birth time?
No — you'll still get a valuable reading without it. The birth hour determines your Hour Pillar (time column), which adds nuance to personality and late-life predictions. If you know the rough time of day (morning/afternoon/evening), even that helps narrow it down.
Why do you need my birth city?
The true solar time — which BaZi uses — varies by longitude. New York and Los Angeles, for example, differ by 3 hours. Birth city lets us calculate your local solar time precisely, which can shift the Hour Pillar and improve accuracy by up to 15%.
How is this different from Western astrology?
Western astrology focuses on planetary positions and zodiac signs. BaZi uses the Chinese lunar calendar and the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) to analyze your chart. BaZi emphasizes timing — specific 10-year cycles and annual luck pillars — making it more actionable for career and relationship planning.
