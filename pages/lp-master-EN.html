FAQ
What is a Four Pillars reading?
An ancient Eastern destiny-analysis method. It maps your birth year, month, day, and hour onto the traditional calendar to reveal your elemental makeup, strengths, challenges, and the timing of key life themes. Think of it as astrology — but with a more precise, systematic framework.
Is it accurate?
Runae calculates your chart precisely from your exact birth details, then uses AI to interpret it in plain English. Interpretations are intended for self-reflection and entertainment — treat them as one perspective alongside your own judgement, not as fact or prediction.
Why do you need my gender?
In the Four Pillars method, gender determines the direction of your 10-year luck pillars. For those born in a Yang year, the cycle direction differs by gender. Without it, the luck-pillar sequence — which governs major life timing — cannot be calculated correctly.
Do I need my exact birth time?
No — you'll still get a valuable reading without it. The birth hour sets your Hour Pillar, which adds nuance to personality and later-life themes. Even a rough time of day (morning / afternoon / evening) helps.
Why do you need my birth city?
True solar time — which the Four Pillars method uses — varies by longitude. Your birth city lets Runae calculate your local solar time, which can shift the Hour Pillar and refine your chart.
Is this AI-generated or done by a real master?
It's AI-generated. Runae's model is built on classical Four Pillars methodology and produces your reading instantly. It's designed to make an ancient system accessible — not to replace personal or professional judgement.
