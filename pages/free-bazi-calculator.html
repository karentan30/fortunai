What you're looking at

What is a Day Master?

In short: Your Day Master is the element of your birth-day pillar — the single element that best represents the core of who you are. It's more specific than a zodiac sun sign because it's computed from your exact birth day, not just your birth month.

In Four Pillars astrology (also called BaZi), your birth moment is broken into four "pillars" — the year, month, day, and hour. Each pillar carries an element. The one that matters most for describing you is the heavenly stem of your day pillar — your Day Master.

There are ten Day Masters in total: a yin and yang version of each of the five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water). Your Day Master is the lens the rest of your chart is read through — which is why two people who share the same zodiac animal can be nothing alike.

Why is BaZi more precise than my zodiac?

Your Western sun sign is set by your birth month. Your Chinese zodiac animal is set by your birth year. BaZi uses all four — year, month, day, and hour — so your chart is far more specific. Your Day Master alone narrows you to 1 of 10 core types; the full four-pillar chart is effectively one of a kind.

Do I need my exact birth time?

No — your Day Master comes from your birth day, so you'll get it either way. Adding your hour fills in the fourth pillar (the hour pillar), which sharpens the timing and relationship parts of a full reading. If you don't know it, select "Unknown" and read the other three pillars.

Is this the same as my zodiac animal?

Not at all. Your animal is only the branch of your year pillar — one of eight chart positions. That's why a shallow "animal compatibility" chart misses most of the picture. When you're ready, the full compatibility reading uses both people's whole charts, not just their animals.
