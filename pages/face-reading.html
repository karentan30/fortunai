The Ma Yi framework

The map of a face

Ma Yi Shen Xiang (麻衣神相) reads the face through three zones, five peaks, and twelve palaces — each governs a domain of life.

🏔

Three Zones (三停)

Upper (forehead) = early & intellect · Middle (brows–nose) = prime years · Lower (mouth–chin) = later life.

⛰

Five Peaks (五岳)

Forehead, nose, left & right cheekbones, and chin — when all five are full and balanced, fortune rises.

🏛

Twelve Palaces (十二宫)

Life Palace · Wealth · Career · Marriage · Children · Friends · Property · Travel · Health · Blessings · Parents and more.

✦

Qi & Spirit (气色神韵)

Luminous complexion and clear eyes are the highest sign — the face brightens when the heart is cultivated.
