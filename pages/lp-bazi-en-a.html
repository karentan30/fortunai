What is BaZi?

Your birth moment, encoded in elements

BaZi (literally "Eight Characters") is a Chinese system that converts your birth date and time into four pairs of characters — each pair representing a pillar of energy from the moment you were born. Think of it as a snapshot of the natural forces at play when you arrived.

Unlike Western astrology which focuses on planetary positions, BaZi works with five elements and how they interact with each other. Your chart reveals which elements you have in abundance, which are missing, and how those imbalances show up in your life patterns — career, relationships, decision-making, and timing.

It doesn't tell you what will happen. It tells you what kind of terrain you're walking through — and when conditions are favorable for big moves.

I don't know my exact birth time. Does that matter? +

Birth time determines the Hour Pillar (~25% of the chart). Without it, you'll get a 3-pillar reading covering Year, Month, and Day — which is still highly accurate for personality, career patterns, and major life cycles.

How is this different from Western astrology or a birth chart? +

Western astrology maps planetary positions against 12 zodiac signs. BaZi maps your birth moment to five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) and analyzes how those elements interact and shift through time. It's more focused on timing, energy cycles, and practical decision-making windows than archetypes.
