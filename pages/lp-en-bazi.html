What are the Four Pillars?
The Four Pillars is an ancient Eastern system of self-discovery with over 3,000 years of recorded use. Your birth date and time generate four "pillars" — year, month, day, and hour. Together they encode your elemental blueprint: five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) and their interactions reveal your innate strengths, challenges, favourable timing for career, and relationship patterns.

Unlike the zodiac you already know — which maps planetary positions in the sky — the Four Pillars is a time-based system. It maps the elemental energies present at the exact moment of your birth, and tracks how those energies interact with the shifting energies of each passing year and decade.
