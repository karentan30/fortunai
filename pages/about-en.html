About Us

EASTERN WISDOM · MODERN CLARITY

☯

Making Eastern wisdom accessible

ShenYuan began with a simple belief: the five thousand years of Eastern metaphysical wisdom shouldn't stay locked in ancient texts or reserved for a handful of masters. When BaZi, Zi Wei, feng shui, and tarot meet modern AI, anyone can reach the spiritual heritage passed down by their ancestors — in the most intuitive way.

"Every encounter is fated."

We're not here to replace the masters who carry this millennia-old tradition. We use AI to make that wisdom universal — so an elder in a village, a professional in a city, or someone far from home overseas can each hold a response from the cosmos in the palm of their hand. This is a warm dialogue between ancient wisdom and future technology.

🎯 Our Mission

Use AI to bring Eastern spiritual culture into everyday life.

We're building a warm, trustworthy spiritual platform. Here, AI isn't a cold algorithm — it becomes a guide who understands destiny: one who understands your uncertainty, and the subtle hints of fate.

Every feature we build is shaped by three core values:

📜
Heritage · Authenticity
All calculations are grounded in authoritative classics (Yuan Hai Zi Ping, the Complete Book of Zi Wei Dou Shu, Plum Blossom Numerology, and others), so every reading rests on a real foundation.
🤖
Technology · Accessibility
AI dramatically lowers the barrier to metaphysical guidance. A master consultation that once cost hundreds is now within everyone's reach, at a fraction of the price.
🌱
Kindness · Giving Back
We take the meaning of "ShenYuan" (good karma, kind connection) seriously — a portion of every purchase flows back to where society needs it most.
🌱 The 20% Giving Pledge
20%
ShenYuan directs 20% of every purchase into the ShenYuan Giving Pool, earmarked to support education for children in underserved areas.
"When you seek the cosmos, the cosmos gives back."
📖 The Story Behind the Pledge

20% isn't a random figure. In Eastern culture, "two" represents the balance of yin and yang, and "twenty" suggests the deliberate space left within completeness — we keep back one fifth of our proceeds and return it to the world.

These funds are managed transparently and directed, through partner charities, toward:

  • 📚 Scholarships — tuition, books, and supplies for children in underserved regions
  • 🏫 Village libraries — small reading corners in less-developed areas
  • 🥣 Nutritious meals — funding hot-meal programs at rural schools

We publish where each contribution goes on the ShenYuan Giving Pool page on a regular basis.
