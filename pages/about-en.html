Reflection, shaped by tradition

Runae brings BaZi, astrology, tarot, face and palm readings into one place. Share the details needed for a reading, choose a tradition, and receive an AI-generated interpretation designed to help you reflect.

A reading can offer a perspective. Your choices remain your own.

Our readings are for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not predictions, and they do not replace professional medical, legal, or financial advice.

✦ How Runae works

Runae uses the birth details or other information you provide to create a reading in the tradition you choose. Face and palm readings require a photo and explain how that image is processed before upload.
