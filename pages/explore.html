Why every reading is real

What the badge means

🟡 Real engine
Your chart is computed, to the exact minute. Four Pillars, Purple Star, Vedic, Maya, Tibetan, Numerology, Love, Compatibility, Cross-Check and more are cast by a precise astronomical / calendar engine — never approximated, never guessed by AI.
🟢 AI reading
An AI interprets your live draw — the way a reader would. Tarot, I-Ching, Fortune Sticks, Moon Blocks, Qi Men and Da Liu Ren are draw-based, so the reading is interpretation, honestly labelled.
🟣 Vision AI
An AI reads your photo. Face & Palm reading trace features and lines from an image you upload — an AI interpretation, for reflection and fun.
