The Ma Yi framework

Lines, mounts & patterns

The Ma Yi school reads your palm through three major lines, eight energy mounts (八丘), and special patterns. Here's the core of what Runae looks at.

❤

Heart line (感情线)

Emotional style, love patterns, and how you connect. Runs across the upper palm from below the little finger.

🧠

Head line (智慧线)

How you think, decide, and process the world. Starts near the index finger and crosses the palm.

🌿

Life line (生命线)

Vitality, life rhythm, and energy reserves. Arcs around the Venus mount. Length ≠ lifespan — the Ma Yi school is clear on this.

✦

Eight mounts (八丘) & fate line

Jupiter, Saturn, Sun, Mercury, Venus, Moon, and Mars mounts — each colors a facet of temperament. The fate line shows career direction.
