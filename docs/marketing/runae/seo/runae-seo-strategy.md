## 0. Where Runae Wins (competitive read — web-validated 2026-08-17)

Live SERPs for the core commercial terms are **crowded with free calculator tools** (masterseanchan, prokerala, bazi-lab, deeporacle, bazicalculators, baguame) and **encyclopedic knowledge sites** (yourchineseastrology, chinahighlights, travelchinaguide). Head terms like `chinese astrology` (100K+) and `bazi calculator` (30K+) are defended by domains with years of authority — **we do not fight these head-on at cold start.**

**Runae's three structural edges** (all defensible, all low-competition):
1. **"AI-decoded, plain-English"** — most incumbents are dense, jargon-heavy, or literal calculators. Runae reads like a person explaining you to yourself. Own the *interpretation* layer, not the *calculation* layer.
2. **"Eastern, not Chinese" self-discovery framing** — reframes BaZi as a **personality/self-knowledge system** (a "better MBTI / better than your zodiac"), which taps a far larger audience than the feng-shui/fortune-telling crowd. Validated: `fiveelementstest.com` and Medium's "Your Day Master is your MBTI" both rank on this exact bridge.
3. **Long-tail + programmatic** — thousands of "born on [date]", "[Day Master] personality", "[zodiac]×[zodiac] compatibility" queries that no single competitor covers comprehensively. This is where a template engine wins outright.

**Bottom line:** attack the **interpretation / self-discovery / long-tail** flanks with content + programmatic pages; use the free Day Master preview as the tool-page magnet; let head terms come later via accumulated authority.

## 4. AI-SEO / GEO — get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, AI Overviews

The behavior shift: users increasingly *ask an AI* instead of clicking blue links. Runae must be the source those AIs quote. Web-validated 2026 tactics:

**A. Crawler access (do this first — free, 30 min)**
- Ensure `robots.txt` / CDN (Caddy on HK) **allows** `GPTBot`, `Google-Extended`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `CCBot`, `Bytespider`. Blocking them = invisible in AI answers.

**B. Publish `llms.txt`** (at `runae.app/llms.txt`)
- Spec-compliant: brand one-liner, what Runae is, method (Four Pillars/BaZi, Eastern, AI-decoded), key definitions (Day Master, Five Elements, four pillars), links to the pillar guides, and the disclaimer. Gives LLMs a clean, authoritative summary to lift. (Optionally `llms-full.txt` with expanded definitions.)

**C. Structured data / schema on every page** (see companion `schema` needs)
- **FAQPage** schema on every blog (the 3–5 FAQs each flagship carries below) — directly feeds AI Overviews + "People Also Ask."
- **Article** schema (author = *Runae Research Team*, datePublished, about).
- **Organization** schema on home (name Runae, sameAs → social profiles) to establish the brand entity.
- **DefinedTerm** / definition blocks for Day Master, Four Pillars, each element.

**D. Answer-first, extractable writing** (baked into all 5 blogs)
- Lead each article + each H2 with a **direct, quotable 2–3 sentence answer**, *then* elaborate. LLMs lift the concise definitional block.
- Use clear H2 **questions** ("What is a Day Master?"), short paragraphs, bulleted lists, comparison tables. Claude/Perplexity favor well-structured, logical content; tables and definition lists get extracted verbatim.
- Include a one-line **"In short:"** / **TL;DR** near the top of each piece.

**E. Entity + off-site presence**
- Consistent brand entity: same Runae description on site, and on social profiles (`sameAs`).
- **Reddit compounds** for Perplexity/Gemini/AI Overviews — a genuine, non-spammy presence in 2–3 relevant subreddits (r/bazi, r/ChineseAstrology, r/astrology-adjacent) over time raises citation odds. (Value-first, disclosed, not link-dropping.)
- Get listed in AI-tool / astrology-tool directories.

**F. Authoritative, honest tone**
- Define terms crisply, cite the tradition's logic, and be honest about determinism ("tendencies, not fate"). LLMs down-rank hype and over-claiming; the honest, precise voice is *also* the most citable voice. This aligns perfectly with Runae's compliance rules — GEO and compliance point the same direction.
