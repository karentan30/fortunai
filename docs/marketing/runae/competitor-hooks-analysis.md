# Runae · Competitor Viral-Hook Intelligence & Taxonomy

> **What this is:** A field-scraped taxonomy of the viral video/ad hooks that win in the divination / astrology / fortune / #spiritualtok space (Co-Star, The Pattern, Nebula, Sanctuary, CHANI, 포스텔러/Forceteller, 점신/Jeomsin, + open #tarottok / #fortunetok / Korean saju / Chinese bazi). Built so **@runae can copy proven formats onto our own features.**
> **Sourcing:** Web research, Aug 2026. Each hook is tagged **[CONFIRMED]** (a source states it) or **[ESTIMATE]** (our inference from genre patterns). No fake numbers — where a metric couldn't be verified it's marked.
> **Brand rules apply:** Runae. Eastern system / Four Pillars / Ten Gods. **ZERO "Chinese" label.** AI-labeled, reflection/entertainment framing, reverse-fate, no fabricated precise stats, no destiny/flattery.

---

## 0. The one-line strategic truth

> **"Astrology apps are 100% a distribution play."** [CONFIRMED — socialgrowthengineers]

The product doesn't win the feed; the **hook + shareable artifact** does. Every app that broke out did it with **UGC + earned/organic virality first, paid seeding second.** Co-Star and The Pattern were pulled up almost entirely by users screenshotting the app and celebrities reacting; Nebula is the outlier that scaled on paid influencer UGC. Runae's job: manufacture the screenshot-able moment (命格卡 / K线 / 身份卡) and wrap it in a proven hook.

## 3. COMPETITOR INFLUENCER PLAYBOOK — who to copy, how to source

Two distinct models exist. **Runae should run the Nebula model for paid + the CHANI model for organic in parallel.**

### The models
- **Organic / founder-led (CHANI, Co-Star, The Pattern):** free value → trusted audience → convert. **CHANI launched profitable day one, ~1.5M downloads, +110% YoY revenue from a "give free, nurture" push.** [CONFIRMED] Co-Star / The Pattern scaled on UGC + earned celebrity moments (Channing Tatum), near-zero paid seeding.
- **Paid / gifting UGC (Nebula, Sanctuary):** Nebula runs the most aggressive program — DMs astrology/spiritual creators, offers **multi-video partnership deals**, they use the app → post #ad UGC (Carrie Berk, @saharrooo). [CONFIRMED]

### Micro vs. mega — the niche skews MICRO
- Trends are **owned by micro/mid niche-creators, not megas.** Named: @jewelsjpj (owns SJPJ), @YourMomsHoroscope (~366K), Indigo Selah (~545K), tarot mids twenty2guru (~312K) / mysticmolly1111 (~256K). **Maren Altman (~1M, "Bitcoin astrologer") is the rare mega.** [CONFIRMED]
- Astrology creators average **~3.5% engagement** and unusually high purchase-trust. [CONFIRMED — Sprout]
- A directory lists **22.3K+ astrology/spiritual TikTok creators with contact info** — a deep, cheap micro pool. [CONFIRMED — SeekSocial]

### How to source & pay (mechanics)
- **Gift-first, then whitelist the winners.** Send free premium Runae → let them post organically → **whitelist / Spark-Ads the top performers** (paid amplification of the exact organic post). Spark Ads report **+142% engagement / +43% conversion** vs non-Spark. Whitelisting adds ~**50–100% on top of base video rate.** [CONFIRMED]
- **Rate benchmark:** ~**$10–100 per 1,000 followers** starting point (a 30K micro ≈ $300–3,000), adjusted for engagement/usage rights. [CONFIRMED]
- **The faceless-account move (highest ROI):** AstroClub runs a portfolio of faceless TikToks (meme slideshows, batch street interviews, zodiac-segmented daily-horoscope accounts) → **~$90K MRR / 80K downloads; one slideshow account hit 1.6M views across 38 posts.** Zodiac-segmented accounts outperformed the general one ~2–3×. [CONFIRMED] **Runae can run this in-house with zero creator fees.**

### App-side cadence to copy
- **Forceteller (포스텔러):** 9M+ users; formal paid-ads program + B2B fortune-content licensing. **Seasonal spike marketing around Lunar New Year (신년운세).**
- **Jeomsin (점신):** rewarded-ad loop ("Lucky Pass": watch 1 ad → ad-free + free fortune) + annual new-year push. [CONFIRMED]
- **Takeaway:** build a **seasonal New-Year / solar-term fortune spike** into Runae's calendar; consider a rewarded-ad free-tier loop.

## 4. RUNAE — THE 3 FORMATS TO COPY FIRST (drop-in)

1. **Identity Triad = "SJPJ done bigger."** Copy the **anti-MBTI wedge (#4) + shareable-TYPE label (#5)**. Hook: *"You know your zodiac. You know your MBTI. You're missing the deepest third layer."* Ship the Ten Gods archetype as **a named type + one screenshot card** (color/element label), serialize "types explained" to farm follows. This is the on-ramp that pulls the massive MBTI/astrology audience into BaZi. **← start here.**
2. **Best Time to Reach Out = the ex-back hook (#9), done honestly.** Copy the reconciliation-timing genre's intent, not its false promises. Hook: *"There are 3 specific dates in the next 30 days it's most likely to land — here's window #1."* Timing guidance, not a guarantee (compliance line). Highest purchase intent in the category.
3. **Life K-Line = the "read me for filth" (#1) + "pick a card" (#6) screenshot moment.** Hook: *"I turned my whole life into a stock chart — and it called out my worst year."* One screenshot-native image (bull/bear years) → "pick which decade you think is yours" → reveal. Portable, faceless, batch-able for the AstroClub-style in-house account portfolio.

**Bonus mechanic (all three):** always end on a participation CTA ("run yours + drop your type/window/worst-year below") and keep the persistent *"for reflection, not fortune-telling · AI"* caption — it satisfies the compliance red line while feeding the comment-loop that the algorithm rewards.
