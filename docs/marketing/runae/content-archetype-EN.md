# Runae · Chart Archetype Content Pack (TikTok + Instagram)

> **Product:** Runae "Your Chart Archetype" — enter your birth details, get the personality pattern your Four Pillars are built around (e.g. **The Strategist / The Catalyst / The Quiet Force / The Alchemist**) + an honest sense of how rare that pattern is. One screenshottable card: *"I'm The Catalyst."*
> **Landing:** `chart-archetype.html` (big archetype card — name + one-line trait + honest rarity band + full reading + share).
> **Growth engine:** status-type identity card, like an MBTI rarity flex — but honest. The screenshottable "I'm a [archetype]" result is the loop: watch → "wait what am I" → run it → get a card → post it / send it to the group chat → they run it → repeat.
> **Brand rules:** Runae. "Eastern chart / Four Pillars." **ZERO "Chinese" label.** Self-discovery framing, AI-labeled, entertainment/reflection only.

> ## 🚨 HONESTY RULES (non-negotiable — these carry the brand)
> - **NO fabricated precise stats.** Do NOT say "1 in 10,000," "only 0.7% of people," "8 in every 10,000." Those numbers are almost always invented. The product itself only gives a **qualitative band** — *common / relatively uncommon / rare* — and the content must match that. On-screen and in captions, say **"most people are X, but this pattern is relatively rare"** — never a fake number.
> - **NO flattery / no destiny.** An archetype is a **neutral personality pattern with a gift AND a shadow.** Never "you're a born winner / destined to succeed." Rarer ≠ better. Always frame it as wiring, not fate.
> - Every result shown is illustrative. Keep the "for reflection, not fortune-telling · AI-generated" tone visible.

> **The honest status hook that works:** curiosity + identity, not "you're special." People share *"I'm The Catalyst and apparently that's a rarer pattern"* the same way they share an MBTI — it's a mirror, not a medal. That's the whole trick, and it's the honest one.
