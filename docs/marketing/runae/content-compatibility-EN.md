# Runae · Compatibility Content Pack (TikTok + Instagram)

> **Product:** Runae compatibility reading — "Do you two actually match?" Enter two birth dates, get a compatibility score + six dimensions (communication, romance, values, conflict, timing, growth) + a written reading you can revisit together.
> **Landing:** `hehun-en.html` (score ring, six dimensions, five elements, "Invite Partner · Unlock Together")
> **Growth engine:** screenshottable result card that users send to their person / their group chat. Same viral loop that made 포스텔러 + 헬로우봇 grow through 궁합 (compatibility) sharing.
> **Brand rules:** Runae. "Eastern compatibility." ZERO "Chinese" label. Self-discovery + relationship. Entertainment / reflection framing, AI-labeled, nothing invented or overstated.
> **Core loop:** watch → curious about your own match → run it → get a card → screenshot → send to your person → they run it → repeat.

## PART 4 — 5 Viral Hook Formulas (fill-in-the-blank)

1. **The Send-Trigger:**
   *"POV: you finally find out if your [situationship / marriage / bestie] is actually compatible — or just [convenient / comfortable / familiar]."*
   → payoff: reveal card, then "sending this to them rn."

2. **The Ex-vs-Current:**
   *"I ran a compatibility reading on my [ex] AND my [current] — the scores explain [everything / our whole timeline / why I left]."*
   → payoff: two rings, the gap, "healing is data now."

3. **The Callout Mirror:**
   *"One line in our compatibility reading was so [disturbingly us / unsettlingly specific / read-us-for-filth] I [screenshotted it before I finished / had to sit down / sent it with no caption]."*
   → payoff: slow-read the line, "how did it KNOW." (Avoid the word "accurate" in relationship copy — ad-policy overstatement risk; use reflection phrasing.)

4. **The Same-Fight:**
   *"Turns out there's a reason we always fight about [the exact same thing / dishes / texting back] — and the [Conflict / Communication] dimension called it out."*
   → payoff: read both styles, "so THAT'S why," tag them.

5. **The Self-Discovery Flip:**
   *"I ran a compatibility check on [everyone I've dated / my best friend / my situationship] and the pattern is [embarrassing / the common denominator is me / not what I expected]."*
   → payoff: montage of scores, self-aware punchline — keeps it self-discovery, not judgment.

## PART 5 — Hashtag Sets + Posting Cadence

**Core set (every post — 4-5 of):**
`#Runae #compatibility #doweematch #relationshiptok #couplegoals`

**Rotating theme sets (pick one to match the post):**
- Situationship/dating: `#situationship #datingtok #dating #relationshipsbelike #tagyourperson`
- Couple/tag: `#couplegoals #coupletok #couplereaction #relationshipcheck #greenflag #redflag`
- Communication: `#communication #lovelanguages #relationshipadvice #whywefight`
- Ex/glow-up: `#exvscurrent #closure #glowup #patterns`
- Self-discovery: `#selfdiscovery #growth #POV #relatable #calledout`

**Volume rule:** TikTok 4-6 hashtags (mix 1 broad + 2-3 mid + 1-2 niche). IG 8-12 (add niche long-tail like `#compatibilitytest`, `#couplecheck`).

**Posting cadence — relationship prime time:**
- **Best windows:** **Friday 6-9pm** and **Saturday 7-10pm** (people together / planning dates / group-chatting) — this is the viral-share sweet spot; lead your strongest "send it to them" hooks here.
- **Secondary:** **Sunday 8-10pm** (Sunday-scaries reflection / "us" recap), **Tuesday–Thursday 12pm & 8pm** (steady reach, test hooks).
- **Weekly rhythm (7 posts/wk suggested):**
  - Mon: self-discovery / softer entry (TT-10 style)
  - Tue: communication/decode (TT-5)
  - Wed: same-fight / relatable (TT-3)
  - Thu: ex-vs-current teaser (TT-4)
  - **Fri (hero):** send-trigger + couple reaction (TT-1 / TT-8)
  - **Sat (hero):** green/red flag + tag-your-person (TT-2)
  - Sun: reflection / one-liner card (TT-9)
- **Format mix per week:** ~4 screen-record reveals, 2 face-reaction, 1 carousel of result cards. Always pin a comment with "link in bio" + the share prompt.
- **Loop reinforcement:** in the first pinned comment, always reply-bait: *"drop your score 👇"* — turns comments into a second share surface.
