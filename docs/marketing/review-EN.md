## 3 Pieces Needing Improvement

### Fix #1 — Jinian Suite 2 IG (Score: 5.4 / 10)
**File:** copy-jinian.md, Suite 2

**Problem:** Emotional copy about New Year blessings from a deceased loved one is derailed mid-caption by product specification language:

> *"Available on iOS & Web | Privacy-first, locally encrypted"*

This is a category error — product specs belong in the bio link or a separate story, never embedded in emotional narrative copy. The hashtag "MysticalTech" is a forced portmanteau that lands in neither the mystical nor tech camp convincingly.

**Fix:**
Remove the spec line entirely. Replace "MysticalTech" with "#AIMemorial" or "#GriefTech" (established Reddit/Twitter communities). The emotional arc should run clean from hook to CTA:

> *"He passed before last New Year. Today, for the first time, I heard him say 'Happy New Year' again.*  
> *Not a memory. A voice — his exact cadence, his warmth. Built from recordings his grandchildren now play every morning.*  
> *Some things are irreplaceable. But presence doesn't have to end.*  
> *→ Voice preservation link in bio"*

Hashtags: #AIMemorial #GriefAndTechnology #DigitalLegacy #ShenYuan #VoicePreservation #RememberingYou #HealingJourney

## Summary Observations

**What's working across the corpus:**
1. The "science bridge" framing (feng shui = environmental psychology, ancestor veneration = neuroplasticity, etc.) is consistently the strongest differentiator. Western audiences who are spirituality-curious but science-literate respond to this approach. Keep it as a mandatory element in all EN copy.
2. Reddit posts that lead with the most skeptical possible framing before pivoting to value consistently outperform posts that try to sell from the first line. The "Probably not [scientifically accurate]. But..." structure appears in 8 of the strongest Reddit posts and should be treated as a template.
3. Anti-promise copy (explicitly stating what ShenYuan does NOT claim to do) is paradoxically the most persuasive brand position in this corpus. The brand is using honesty as a conversion mechanism, which is exactly right for this audience.

**Systemic issues to fix across all EN copy:**
1. **Hashtag QA is inconsistent.** Three separate pieces had hashtag errors (truncation, wrong category, typo). Implement a mandatory hashtag review pass before any copy leaves production: check spelling, verify category relevance, spot-check that target hashtags are active on platform.
2. **Product spec language leaks into emotional copy** (Jinian S2 is the worst case, but similar micro-interruptions appear elsewhere). Establish a rule: no product features (pricing, platform availability, tech specs) inside narrative captions. That information belongs in the bio, link, or story highlights.
3. **IG hashtag counts are sometimes too high** (14–16 tags) for 2024+ algorithm preferences. Trim to 8–10 maximum per post. Quality over quantity.
